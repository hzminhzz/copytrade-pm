### Milestone: `v1.0-combo2-paper - One-shot paper tracking engine`

**Issue 1 - Implement and verify the entire Combo 2 five-minute paper tracker end to end**

**Goal:** Complete every acceptance criterion in the approved `spec.md` through independently reviewed changes and one engineering acceptance report. A foundation-only merge must not close this issue; it remains open until the entire prospective paper service is verified.

**Implementation checkpoints (internal, not separate GitHub tickets):**

1. **Frozen contract and no-mutation guarantee:** Versioned manifest for roster and nominal $5,000 paper account; reject invalid/missing account IDs, conflicting source snapshots, unconfirmed contract interpretation or any live-order mode. Paper operator can inspect read-only APIs only; no outbound order/position/leverage mutation functions are reachable.
2. **Durable state:** Reuse PM-03's audited, idempotent, crash-safe event store. Persist original source event time, locally first-received time, decision time, synthetic-fill time, due batch, paper inventory, journal intent, approval, risk latch and event hash. Replay after crash is identical and cannot duplicate an order.
3. **Read-only source:** Observe W028, CA-008, W015 exactly, including correct subaccount/DEX, live equity and full signed positions via read-only clearinghouseState. Missing/inconsistent equity or positions, incomplete DEX census and source/reconnect gaps fail closed; never treat unknown as zero, never retrospectively invent first-known events.
4. **Five-minute batching:** First locally received eligible event at T fixes T+300s; subsequent events do not reset the deadline. At due time use latest coherent as-of leader equity and positions, collapse fills into one net external paper action per exact instrument, and preserve separate manager attribution. Cold-start snapshots do not automatically backfill positions.
5. **Market mapping:** Verified canonical source venue/DEX/contract to Propr mapping and precision; fail closed or mark PARTIAL_BOOK on unsupported instruments. Distinguish market-open/closed and stale/unknown marks. Do not guess market IDs or HIP-3 aliases.
6. **Sizing and gross cap:** `manager_desired_notional = signed_leader_qty * leader_mark / current_leader_equity * $1,000`. With source $100k equity and $300k BTC long, target is $3k **before** clamp; cap at $1,250 after summing absolute manager BTC notionals, proportionally preserve signed contributions, and **only then** net manager orders.
7. **Consolidated risk:** Enforce $5k portfolio manager-gross, margin/stress/concentration and hedge-break guards. Latch soft freezes at daily intrabar loss reaching $120 on a $5k daily-start balance (scale with actual paper start-of-day balance) or initial-to-current paper drawdown reaching $240. Reset 00:00 UTC based on prior paper realized balance with explicit operator reconciliation; total/hard breach remains latched. Allow executable risk reductions but no additions after freeze.
8. **Delta, minimum and reversals:** Use allowed target minus **filled synthetic paper inventory**, not real Propr holdings or staged intents; opening gate is $10 and precision/lot minimum. Full closes bypass minimum when executable; dust remains visible. Split reversal into reduce-only close and separately risk-gated opening. Cross-manager virtual netting cannot create fictitious external fills.
9. **Hypothetical fill and accounting:** Model fees, slippage, funding, latency, capacity, rejections and partial fills; preserve pending residuals and mark-to-market paper cash/equity. Reconcile the consolidated ledger with per-manager attribution. Staging in local SQLite is not a fill or live order.
10. **Closed-market valuation:** Market closure prevents simulated new orders; bounded previous-mark carry is labeled valuation only. Unknown/unbounded stale price freezes new risk. Reopen jumps and adverse 1m intrabar marks flow into the paper DD; do not claim tick fidelity.
11. **Read-only Propr adapter:** Integrate account #JhW9 as a distinct **read-only monitor** and project hypothetical intents to the local SQLite `IntentJournal`, with a thin `trading-connector/connectors/propr/examples/paper_track_combo2.py` entrypoint. Any connector edits require a separate linked connector PR, not hidden copying into this repo; preserve its pre-existing untracked file and dirty changes. Never call Propr order-create/cancel/close/replace/leverage endpoints.
12. **Operator report and runbook:** Provide source age and status, due batches, desired-vs-capped-vs-allowed-vs-executed per-wallet exposure and tracking error, total and daily paper losses, latches, fee/funding, skipped assets, actual Propr monitor as a separate line, restart/reconciliation instructions. Explicit HYPOTHETICAL/NOT SCIENTIFICALLY QUALIFIED status.
