# ADR: Authentic ANOS real-world E2E evidence and independent verdicts

**Date:** 2026-11-14

**Status:** Accepted design, PARKED under #101 (owner-approved Q1–Q21, 2026-11-14). E2E grill STOPPED; no RW01–RW08 pack/oracle/trace implementation tickets are authorized.

**Scope:** ANOS read-only agent assessment in `copytrade-pm` and its companion read-only owner interfaces. This is not test implementation, service deployment, source-policy activation, scientific qualification or trading authorization.

## Context

The merged ANOS engineering acceptance scenarios S1–S8 use controlled fixtures to verify contracts. They do not by themselves prove that an operating agent correctly interprets original production observations. Historical source journals, sealed portfolio membership, virtual-paper accounting, installed processes and protected Guardian state belong to different authorities and have different clocks. Current observations change while historical incidents must remain independently reproducible.

The retired V4 first-known T0+300 policy is not a prospective target. The sole prospective source-policy target is CWSA's official five-minute REST checkpoints. Real-world E2E must preserve authentic originals, protect live journals and keep a blocked financial conclusion truthful.

## Decision — owner-approved Round 1

1. **Immutable originals, no live replay mutation (Q1=A).** Historical scenarios consume frozen read-only evidence packs derived from actual owner originals, with verifiable identity, provenance and original hashes. Originals remain private on the VPS. Only redacted manifests, original hashes, UTC timestamps and non-sensitive references may enter Git. Replay interprets evidence through its owning reader and must not write to live source/paper/Guardian journals or create new prospective effects. The exact snapshot-atomicity, pinning and retention contract remains to be specified.
2. **Mandatory workflow, conditional protected originals (Q2=B).** RW01–RW07 are mandatory real-world scenario targets. RW08 must exercise the Guardian/intent/reconciliation *boundary* and correct abstention; an authentic protected Guardian original is required only if independently authorized and accessible. Missing genuine evidence is explicitly `BLOCKED_EVIDENCE`, never filled with a fixture or counted as fully proven original coverage. Mandatory case completion is not asserted when required originals are missing.
3. **Two separate verdict dimensions (Q3=A).** Agent-workflow verdicts describe correct discovery, provenance, scoped explanation, null handling, abstention and safe operation: `WORKFLOW_PASS`, `WORKFLOW_FAIL` or `WORKFLOW_BLOCKED_EVIDENCE`. Owner operational/source/financial readiness and scientific/trading authority are separate dimensions. Correctly reporting `BLOCKED_EVIDENCE`, `UNKNOWN` or `NOT_AUTHORIZED` can be `WORKFLOW_PASS` if the underlying case evidence is sufficient to validate that answer; it never promotes financial readiness.
4. **Native owners and versioned protocol (Q4=A).** The proposed `anos_realworld_e2e_v1` is a reusable versioned agent procedure, evidence-manifest contract and independent verdict rubric, not another operator daemon, financial service or authority. It discovers and calls existing read-only capabilities, then preserves original owner references and consumer versions. It does not operate or authorize broker actions. Keep the 13 `copytrade_agent_tasks_v1` tasks, eight historical ANOS engineering scenarios S1–S8, eight new RW01–RW08 real-world evidence scenarios and 15 ANOS engineering tickets distinct.

## Decision — owner-approved Round 2

5. **Minimal complete pack and consistent capture (Q5=A).** Select only the owner-original records needed for the case, but include complete verification ancestry, immutable source heads, necessary authentic REST request/response bytes, account/scope/roster/portfolio seals and applicable paper proof. Historical source, paper, registry and protected records must be captured as consistent snapshots within each authority, never by reading incoherent SQLite/WAL fragments or editing live originals. Cross-owner records are **not** one atomic snapshot: preserve each native cutoff and head separately. The capture implementation and retention duration are later design decisions.
6. **Producer and verifier both pinned (Q6=A).** Record the exact historical producer wire schema, source policy, software provenance and original head independently from the current ANOS verifier/consumer revision and actual deployed process. Validate original identity against the producing authority's versioned semantics before interpreting it with the current read-only contract. Unsupported migration or conflicting semantics is a reported incompatibility or evidence block, never silent rewriting or using current `main` as proof of earlier behavior.
7. **Generation-bounded incidents (Q7=A).** Each case is bound to one verifiable as-of portfolio selection, original roster/seal, selected account/DEX scope, policy epoch and source generation. A legitimate boundary transition creates another explicitly linked case, not a retroactive reinterpretation of the earlier incident. Do not infer historical membership from a present-day portfolio or copy a later known alias backward.
8. **Meaning-bearing owner-head parity (Q8=A).** CLI, Ops Control Center HTTP and browser conclusions are comparable only when all relevant owner heads, exact scope, inspection mode, producer/consumer contracts and evidence clocks agree. For a frozen pack, use identical originals. For independent live read-only observations, changed heads or comparable-scope uncertainty yields `INCOMPARABLE_LIVE_HEADS` (an evaluation label, not a native owner result); a bounded refresh may supply a comparable pair. Divergence on truly identical verified heads is `WORKFLOW_FAIL`. Neither a new display time nor healthy PID refreshes an old source or finance head.
9. **Protected Guardian original-owner projection (Q9=A).** If independently authorized, use the existing read-only Guardian journal-chain verification and `operator.intent`, `operator.risk` and `operator.broker` projections from the same verified owner state. Preserve original intent identity, dispatch certainty, reservation/HALT/floors, release and compatibility pins. No protected journal originals are copied into general ANOS storage or Git. Unavailable, inconsistent or unauthenticated protected evidence remains `UNKNOWN` or `BLOCKED_EVIDENCE`, never a synthetic rejection or free retry.
10. **Case-derived negative controls (Q10=A).** Each RW01–RW08 scenario has at least one isolated, explicitly `NEGATIVE_CONTROL`-labeled falsifier derived from that case's verified accessible originals, with known targeted invalidation and expected rejection/abstention. Test wrong bytes/hash, reordered clocks, changed selected membership, fabricated zero economics, unsafe UI permission, unbound process, invalid cache provenance and unverified Guardian terminality as applicable. For conditional RW08 without protected originals, derive the boundary falsifier from authentic accessible source/paper evidence and the *verified absence* of Guardian proof: never claim an authentic protected incident or create a fake one. Altered copies are not replacements for authentic case evidence; original snapshots remain unchanged.

**Implementation boundary:** These are architectural constraints on a future versioned E2E procedure, not confirmation that any snapshot, tester, capture signer, negative-control pack or runtime cutover has been created.

## Decision — owner-approved Round 3

11. **Consistent private capture and reviewed retention (Q11=A).** Freeze authentic owner inputs with transactionally consistent SQLite snapshots (prefer the owner-compatible SQLite online backup mechanism when capturing a live WAL store) and SHA-256 verification of the original external request/response bytes and ancestry. Use private restricted storage, independent integrity receipts, and no modifications to the original running journal. Retain an accepted case until explicit reviewed retirement, not automatic TTL expiry. Treat the proposed capture mechanism as design: no pack has been captured by this ADR.
12. **One versioned manifest vocabulary (Q12=A).** The future `anos_realworld_e2e_manifest_v1` provides shared case identity, requested operation and scope, as-of, original record hashes and heads, source/registry/paper/Guardian authority-specific references, producer/consumer revision and schema pins, multiple original clocks, verification method, redaction/custody reference and expected evidence requirements. Keep owner-specific proofs in their own typed sections; never flatten independent authorities into one fabricated global version or include private original paths and credentials in Git. The exact required-field schema remains design work.
13. **Independent preregistered oracle (Q13=A).** An independent reviewer verifies original authority evidence, settles the expected supported/blocked conclusion and case falsifier before the evaluated agent runs, and freezes the answer key separately from the agent-visible pack. The agent cannot read or author its own grading key. A native ANOS read-only projection is evidence to verify, not the sole independent truth oracle.
14. **Two distinct parity checks (Q14=A).** For historical RW05, a frozen authentic pack feeds isolated CLI, read-only HTTP and browser surfaces under declared pinned versions. Independently, live GET-only checks inspect the deployed Ops Control Center and independently produced native CLI evidence, with original owner heads, scope and clocks recorded. Only equal verified meaning-bearing heads are directly comparable; races and incompatible heads are explicitly incomparable, not false mismatch passes or fresh financial proof.
15. **Host-captured genuine agent and full-suite criteria (Q15=A).** To claim an actual agent passed RW01–RW08, capture its attempted and executed tool events plus final answer through an independently administered, externally pinned/signed host recorder and an independent answer oracle. Create a **separate** versioned RW workload; do not modify or relabel the 13-task frozen `copytrade_agent_tasks_v1` benchmark or its evaluation claims. Full authentic suite PASS requires verifiable genuine RW01–RW07 positives, RW08 correct protected-owner boundary/abstention, required authentic-case falsifiers, and no fabricated authority, unsupported positive, prohibited access or unsafe operation. A correctly blocked individual workflow does not make missing mandatory genuine evidence disappear. Individual behavior verdict and suite coverage remain separate.
16. **CI/private/live execution isolation (Q16=A).** Public CI runs deterministic contract checks and redacted/synthetic negative tests only. Authentic original-evidence and genuine agent runs execute in an isolated private **read-only** VPS evaluation context, without inheriting production writer privileges, with separately approved protected-reader access if any. Current live observations are an explicitly requested GET/read-only mode, not an always-on source probe or every-push private CI dependency. No broker credentials, order route or signed trading mandate are introduced by testing.

**Implementation boundary:** No snapshot capture, host capture signer, deployed browser runner or E2E test implementation is implied by accepting Q11–Q16.

## Decision — owner-approved Round 4 and parked disposition

17. **Two linked manifests (Q17=A).** Publish only a safe redacted versioned index in Git, cryptographically bound by digest to an owner-private custody record with original verification locators and capture receipts. Neither grants authority or substitutes for owner originals.
18. **Semantic claim oracle (Q18=A).** Independently preregister exact structured identities, SHA values, times, states and authorization fields, plus claim predicates, required negations, scoped blockers and prohibited conclusions. Allow semantically equivalent explanatory prose; do not require a single memorized golden paragraph. Keep the oracle independent and unavailable to the evaluated agent.
19. **One bounded live-head retry (Q19=A).** Compare all meaning-bearing owner heads before claiming CLI/Site parity. Account for the installed eight-second owner-view cache; allow no more than one bounded re-observation. If heads remain unequal or scope/contract incomparable, emit `INCOMPARABLE_LIVE_HEADS` as an evaluation status, neither PASS nor FAIL. Divergence on the same verified heads is FAIL. Never poll indefinitely.
20. **Eight individually isolated agent runs (Q20=A).** Each RW01–RW08 model-led task receives only its reviewed operation scope and evidence access; it has an independent host trace and independent adjudication. Do not leak previous scenario answers or the hidden oracle into subsequent runs. An optional combined investigation never replaces individual case acceptance.
21. **Enforced read-only execution (Q21=A).** Pre-authorize and constrain capabilities and filesystem access; prohibit broker/order/mutation credentials; verify hashes of immutable captured packs before and after replay, inspect every attempted tool operation, and keep live owner journals outside the evaluator's write scope. Naturally advancing live journal bytes are not a violation; evaluator-boundary proof is required. A declared `READ_ONLY` label alone is insufficient.

**Parked disposition:** These Q1–Q21 decisions constitute a reusable benchmark design record associated with [#101](https://github.com/hzminhzz/copytrade-pm/issues/101), not completion of #101's existing 13-task independent host-capture acceptance. Preserve the original benchmark and unresolved `BLOCKED_NO_INDEPENDENT_HOST_TRACE`. Do not create a further RW01–RW08 ticket wave or implement pack/oracle/trace tooling, run an agent benchmark, or start another grill round without a separate owner request. The separately authorized CWSA/OPS release cutover is operational work outside this ADR.

## Trade-offs and rejected alternatives

- Reading mutable live historical journal heads without immutable snapshots would not give a reproducible incident proof; rejected for historical replay, though independently timestamped current read-only observations remain required.
- Requiring protected original Guardian access for every E2E run would unnecessarily couple source workflow verification to protected credentials; rejected. Protected authority is never inferred when absent.
- A single green readiness score would conflate agent reasoning with operational/financial admission; rejected.
- A separate orchestration engine would duplicate existing operation, owner and journal boundaries; rejected.
- Fixture-generated successes can be negative controls and engineering checks, but cannot be presented as authentic wallet, source or financial proof.
- Whole-store duplication per case, current-code reinterpretation of historical records, inferred historical membership, and false parity between advancing live snapshots were rejected in favor of minimal ancestry, dual version pins, generation boundaries and head-aware comparability.
- Copying protected Guardian journals into general ANOS evidence storage was rejected in favor of owner-verified, read-only projections.
- Ordinary filesystem copies of mutable WAL files, auto-expiring accepted evidence, ad hoc scenario manifests, model-self-graded answers, live-only parity, unsigned/model-authored traces as agent proof, and private/live tests on every CI push were rejected.
- Exposing private locators in Git, literal-text-only grading, unbounded polling on advancing source heads, one shared answer-contaminated agent run, and unverified `READ_ONLY` assertions were rejected.

## Consequences and guardrails

- Historical evidence and current live observations are complementary, not interchangeable. New reads must preserve original first-known, checkpoint, publication, decision and finance clocks individually.
- Verify the actual installed revision and running process independently from Git `main`. Historical module names alone do not establish active source policy.
- Preserve per-wallet and per-portfolio fault scope. A healthy source does not fill missing financial inputs; missing economics remain `null`.
- Protected Guardian outcomes, reservations and HALT are never overwritten or inferred from paper simulations. All evaluation operations are read-only.
- Reproducible agent-behavior claims must use the pre-existing independent host-trace standard; signed fixtures still demonstrate engineering behavior only. The existing `copytrade_agent_tasks_v1` trace evaluator is explicitly restricted to its 13 frozen tasks and cannot, unchanged, score RW01–RW08 as real-agent E2E. The new protocol must retain the frozen workload's identity and separate empirical claims.
- **Parked implementation details, not current decisions:** precise schema/validation, case-specific owner joins and expected outputs, executable negative controls, retention implementation, independent host capture, and full evaluation runbook remain unimplemented. No genuine RW case or independent model run is claimed by this design acceptance. This is a documented stopping point, not an invitation to restart the grill.
