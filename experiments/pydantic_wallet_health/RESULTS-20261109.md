## Review failures and repairs

The initial focused suite passed 21 tests. Direct diff review then identified missing adversarial cases:

1. A correct label with inadequate required citations was not counted as a false confident claim. A separate unsupported edge claim also escaped the supported-claim predicate. The new support regression failed; the predicate now requires complete oracle support and no edge claim.
2. Large offsetting decimal cash flows depended on ambient Decimal precision. A low-precision cancellation regression failed. The bounded local 128-digit arithmetic now preserves exact reconciliation.
3. The small-sample p95 calculation rounded down. The runner now uses nearest-rank ceiling, with a boundary regression covering 16 and one sample.
4. Fully offsetting negative fees and positive funding were called a gain. The new zero-net-flow regression failed; the diagnostic now reports mixed flows.
5. A zero-equity, zero-activity wallet was called active. The new empty-wallet regression failed; the diagnostic now abstains.

The final 26-test suite includes the repaired cases, real CLI execution, future-evidence exclusion, old liquidation/window rejection, original SHA and duplicate-key rejection, nonfinite/coercion rejection, explicit authority rejection, signature/corpus/input binding, and a child-process proof that the network guard rejects connection attempts.
